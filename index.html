Who We Are

An Established Leader in Industrial Contracting

Our Journey

Decades of Growth & Innovation

1974

The Foundation

Founder Ken Gottstein began his construction industry career, developing an unmatched work ethic and deep appreciation for craftsmanship under experienced mentors.

1984

Gottstein Corporation Launches

The corporation officially launched in Hazle Township, PA, serving manufacturers in local industrial parks with a team of skilled managers and technicians.

1990

Early Growth

Grew to five tradesmen operating from a 1,000 sq ft shop, offering welding, fabrication, plumbing systems, and equipment installation. Later expanded to a 2,500 sq ft facility.

2003

Major Expansion

Expanded to a 7-acre parcel in Humboldt Industrial Park with a 9,000 sq ft fabrication shop. Service area grew to include New York, New Jersey, Connecticut, and Maryland.

2010

West Coast Operations

Opened Bakersfield, California location with a 3,000 sq ft fabrication shop, extending services across California, Arizona, Colorado, Texas, and Washington.

2014

Southern & International Reach

Expanded to Dallas, Texas and southern U.S. regions. Began international projects in Canada and Mexico, establishing a true North American presence.

2020

Innovation & New Capabilities

Added life-sustaining business capabilities, general contracting, engineering management, CNC machining, water jet technology, and prototype services.
